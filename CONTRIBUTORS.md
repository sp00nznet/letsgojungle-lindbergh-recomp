## Issue reports and testing

### [@Redmoneydash](https://github.com/Redmoneydash)
Reported, in #1, a native PS5 port of *Initial D Arcade Stage 4* built on
lindberghrecomp
([initiald4-lindbergh-ps5-port](https://github.com/Redmoneydash/initiald4-lindbergh-ps5-port)).
It was the first Lindbergh title brought up on the toolkit outside this
project. Their patch found that the Cg shader table was too small, which is now
fixed in the toolkit (lindberghrecomp#1). They also narrowed the Cg titles'
missing 3D pass down to the offscreen composite.

## A note on AI-assisted contributions

PRs written with AI help are welcome, provided a person understood the change
